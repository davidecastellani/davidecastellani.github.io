## Publications

This site includes a placeholder for publications. Two options:

1. Keep a `publications.bib` BibTeX file and render it with a static generator or custom script (not implemented here).
2. Manually list publications below in markdown.

### Example (manual)

1. D. Castellani, A. Researcher. "Example paper title." Journal Name, 2025.


To use BibTeX, paste your `.bib` content in `publications.bib` and tell me if you want me to add a simple script/plugin to render it.
